# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers own the readiness and coordination of production launches. They ensure that new features, fixes, and upgrades are validated, communicated, and delivered with clear ownership across the project team.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release schedules, deployment windows, and launch readiness checks
- Work with engineering, QA, and PMs to confirm acceptance criteria and launch criteria are met
- Prepare release notes, stakeholder notifications, and rollback plans
- Track post-deployment verification and issue triage after go-live
- Balance business urgency with operational stability and risk reduction

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and deployment disruption
- Improve predictability and clarity around launch timing
- Maintain a reliable handoff from delivery to support and stakeholder communication

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and launch checklists
- Deployment status updates to PMs, stakeholders, and support teams
- Post-release follow-ups and incident coordination when needed

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Project Managers to align timelines and approvals
- Partners with Developers and QA to confirm readiness and smoke-test success
- Coordinates with Product Managers on launch messaging and stakeholder expectations
- Escalates deployment or rollback risks to the PM and Product Lead as needed

---

## Technical Architects / Engineering Leads

### Role Summary
Technical Architects and Engineering Leads provide technical direction, standards, and design guidance. They help teams make sound architecture decisions while balancing delivery speed, maintainability, and risk.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture, integration patterns, and standards
- Review design trade-offs and key technical decisions with product and delivery leads
- Identify technical dependencies, constraints, and risk areas early
- Support estimation, sequencing, and solution design for complex work
- Guide the technical quality bar for maintainability, scalability, and security

### Goals
- Align implementation decisions with strategic technical direction
- Reduce avoidable rework and delivery risk
- Ensure systems remain sustainable and supportable over time

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews, design docs, and technical decision records
- Planning discussions with PMs and Product Managers
- Dependency tracking and escalation for high-risk technical work

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Product Managers to assess feasibility and trade-offs
- Advises Project Managers on dependency planning and delivery risks
- Guides Developers on design patterns, standards, and technical debt handling
- Escalates architecture or platform risks that could affect schedule, scope, or quality

---

## Security / Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads ensure that projects meet required controls, privacy expectations, and risk standards before release. They help protect the business, customers, and technical systems from unnecessary exposure.

### Responsibilities
- Review project work against security, privacy, and compliance requirements
- Identify access controls, data handling, and audit requirements early in the lifecycle
- Support threat modeling, risk assessments, and secure design reviews
- Partner with engineering on remediation and policy adherence
- Help define approval gates for sensitive or regulated work

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk across delivery efforts
- Improve trust, resilience, and audit readiness
- Prevent late-stage blockers by integrating controls earlier

### Typical Communication
- Security review sessions and dependency tracking
- Risk summaries for PMs, Product Leads, and leadership
- Escalation with incident and compliance workflows when needed

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to ensure requirements and priorities account for compliance needs
- Coordinates with Project Managers on risk tracking and release gating
- Advises Developers and Technical Leads on secure implementation patterns
- Supports Release Managers by validating launch readiness and rollback considerations

---

## Customer Success / Stakeholder Liaisons

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Stakeholder Liaisons connect project delivery with customer needs, stakeholder expectations, and business context. They translate feedback and external signals into actionable priorities for the team.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and communicate customer feedback, usage signals, and stakeholder concerns
- Align project updates, milestones, and release communications with stakeholder needs
- Help prioritize features or improvements based on business and user impact
- Coordinate escalations and dependency communication across internal and external groups
- Support adoption, training, and readiness planning where relevant

### Goals
- Improve customer value and stakeholder confidence
- Ensure product decisions reflect real-world needs and feedback
- Strengthen communication and alignment between team execution and business stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly stakeholder updates, customer feedback sessions, and issue triage
- Cross-functional alignment meetings with PMs and Product Managers
- Release messaging and adoption readiness discussions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Feeds customer insight into Product Manager prioritization and backlog refinement
- Supports Project Managers with communication plans and milestone updates
- Collaborates with Release Managers on launch readiness and stakeholder messaging
- Helps Developers and Engineers understand impact of user pain points or adoption blockers

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- When a project includes cross-functional work, teams may add or adapt personas to fit delivery context, stakeholder maturity, and operational complexity.
