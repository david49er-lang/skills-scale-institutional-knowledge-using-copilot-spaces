# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Business Sponsor

### Role Summary
Business Sponsors provide strategic sponsorship for the initiative, approve necessary investment, and ensure work advances the organization’s goals. They act as the decision-maker for business priorities and high-level trade-offs.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm the business case and desired outcomes
- Approve scope, budget, and priority trade-offs
- Align stakeholders around project goals and timeline expectations
- Support issue escalation when decisions or resources are needed
- Champion adoption and measurable impact after release

### How they interact with existing roles
- With Product Managers: align on business value, strategic goals, and priority trade-offs
- With Project Managers: review progress against milestones and resource needs
- With Developers and Technical Leads: clarify outcomes and business constraints without becoming involved in day-to-day delivery decisions
- With Stakeholders: communicate decisions, expectations, and success criteria

### Goals
- Ensure the project produces meaningful business value
- Keep cross-functional teams aligned on outcomes and direction
- Support timely decisions that unblock delivery

### Typical Communication
- Steering committee updates
- Monthly or milestone-based reviews
- Decision and escalation discussions

---

## Engineering Manager / Technical Lead

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers or Technical Leads turn product direction into executable delivery plans. They coordinate engineering capacity, technical trade-offs, and execution quality across the development team.

### Responsibilities
- Plan engineering work and align delivery with roadmap milestones
- Manage technical dependencies, risks, and scheduling constraints
- Support architecture, design, and implementation decisions
- Coordinate team capacity, staffing, and work prioritization
- Partner with Product and Project Managers on realistic delivery commitments

### How they interact with existing roles
- With Developers: provide technical guidance, prioritization, and feedback on feasibility
- With Product Managers: translate priorities into delivery plans and technical constraints
- With Project Managers: align timelines, dependencies, and escalation paths
- With QA leads: ensure quality gates, testability, and release readiness are built into the plan

### Goals
- Deliver stable, maintainable technical outcomes
- Reduce delivery friction and unplanned rework
- Balance speed, quality, and engineering sustainability

### Typical Communication
- Engineering planning meetings
- Architecture and design reviews
- Delivery status updates and capacity discussions

---

## QA Lead / Test Manager

### Role Summary
QA Leads define the quality strategy for the project and ensure that features are validated against acceptance criteria and release readiness requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing scope, strategy, and quality gates
- Coordinate test planning for unit, integration, and end-to-end validation
- Validate that work meets acceptance criteria and business expectations
- Flag quality risks early and support defect triage
- Partner on release sign-off and production readiness checks

### How they interact with existing roles
- With Developers: review testability, defects, and release readiness
- With Product Managers: validate acceptance criteria and user-facing quality expectations
- With Project Managers: track QA status, blockers, and release readiness
- With Security or Compliance reviewers: confirm risk treatment and secure deployment checks

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and defects in production
- Maintain confidence in quality before launch
- Ensure quality expectations are visible and measurable

### Typical Communication
- Test planning sessions
- Defect triage and sign-off meetings
- Release readiness reviews

---

## Security / Compliance Reviewer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Reviewers ensure work meets internal policies, regulatory expectations, and secure delivery standards. They help protect the organization from risk while keeping projects moving.

### Responsibilities
- Review architecture, features, and changes for security and compliance risks
- Define required controls, reviews, and approval checkpoints
- Support secure deployment, data handling, and incident response readiness
- Partner with teams on remediation planning when risks are identified
- Confirm that release and operational requirements meet policy standards

### How they interact with existing roles
- With Developers: review design decisions, threat models, and secure implementation practices
- With Project Managers: confirm gating requirements, approvals, and dependency timing
- With Business Sponsors: communicate risk exposure and necessary trade-offs
- With Release leads and QA: ensure checks are in place before production rollout

### Goals
- Reduce risk of security or compliance failures
- Enable safe, policy-aligned delivery
- Provide a clear path to remediation when issues are found

### Typical Communication
- Security review checkpoints
- Risk and control discussions
- Release and incident readiness review

---

## Customer Success / Support Representative

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Support representatives bring user and support experience back into the project. They help the team understand friction, adoption issues, and post-release impacts.

### Responsibilities
- Capture customer feedback, pain points, and unmet needs
- Escalate recurring issues and customer-impacting risks
- Help validate whether a release is delivering expected value
- Contribute to rollout communication, enablement, and adoption support
- Share operational insights that influence backlog and roadmap adjustments

### How they interact with existing roles
- With Product Managers: provide feedback about customer needs and feature value
- With Project Managers: highlight support-related risks, dependencies, and stakeholder concerns
- With Developers and QA: clarify bug patterns and product gaps that require fixes
- With Business Sponsors: provide evidence of customer impact and adoption progress

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and retention
- Surface operational issues before they become major blockers
- Ensure the team learns from real usage and support patterns

### Typical Communication
- Feedback loops and customer issue reviews
- Release readiness and adoption check-ins
- Support escalation and incident communication

---

## Data / Analytics Owner

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Owners help teams define, measure, and evaluate success metrics so decisions are informed by evidence rather than assumptions.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics, reporting cadence, and measurement approach
- Validate instrumentation and data quality for product and project decisions
- Monitor adoption, performance, and customer impact after release
- Partner with Product and PM teams on dashboarding and reporting
- Highlight where outcomes are not meeting expectations and suggest follow-up action

### How they interact with existing roles
- With Product Managers: translate business goals into measurable outcomes and KPIs
- With Project Managers: report progress against milestones and measurable value delivery
- With Developers and QA: validate that tracking and monitoring support release decisions
- With Business Sponsors: show the impact of the initiative on business metrics and customer outcomes

### Goals
- Make outcomes visible and measurable
- Support evidence-based iteration and decision-making
- Improve visibility into adoption, quality, and business value

### Typical Communication
- Reporting and dashboard reviews
- Milestone and launch metrics reviews
- Operational and business impact updates
